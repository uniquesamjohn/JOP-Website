About Us

Established on the core commitment of delivering trusted assessment enabled by technology and efficiently delivered with integrity.

Identity & Purpose

Who We Are

JOP CBT Center is envisioned to be a premier, highly trusted digital assessment and examination services organisation, providing dependable testing infrastructure and digital administrative solutions in Nigeria and beyond.

Our facility was established to address the critical need for credible, technologically sound, and secure computer-based assessment venues. As examinations transition globally from manual paper processes to electronic administration, educational institutions, professional certification boards, and corporate recruiting bodies demand testing centres with uncompromised integrity, dependable power architecture, and disciplined supervision.

At JOP CBT Center, our operations are anchored upon our corporate business proposition: “Trusted assessment enabled by technology, efficiently delivered with integrity.” We combine modern computing workstations with structured candidate handling, uninterrupted power redundancy, and transparent invigilation protocols to provide an optimal evaluation environment for every candidate.
