Verified Technical Specifications

Infrastructure Designed for Performance

Every layer of the JOP CBT Centre facility in Abeokuta is engineered to prevent downtime, safeguard examination integrity, and maintain optimal candidate comfort.

Examination Workstations

Equipped with 250 primary examination workstations and approximately ~50 additional backup computers in individual privacy cubicles with clear numbering and wide invigilation aisles.

Backup Power Systems

24/7 tri-layered power redundancy combining roof-mounted hybrid solar installations, industrial diesel generators, and central Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) units for zero interruption.

Network Connectivity

High-availability dedicated internet connectivity paired with structured gigabit local area networking engineered for low-latency synchronization with examination servers.

CCTV Monitoring

Comprehensive internal and perimeter closed-circuit television (CCTV) surveillance covering the testing floor, entrance checkpoints, holding areas, and corridors.

Candidate Verification

Biometric identity authentication terminals and controlled perimeter screening procedures to prevent candidate impersonation and protect examination credibility.

Technical Support Facilities

Dedicated server administration room, climate-controlled air conditioning across the testing hall, wheelchair-accessible compound ramps, and on-site technical proctors.
