Our Resources & Infrastructure

An advanced examination facility purpose-built with 250 testing stations, multi-tier power redundancy, continuous security monitoring, and high-reliability connectivity.

Facility Architecture

Engineered for Assessment Integrity

Every layer of our infrastructure is engineered to prevent disruption, safeguard exam credibility, and foster an optimal testing environment.

Capacity

250-Seater CBT Center

Our central testing hall provides a verified seating capacity of 250 concurrent assessment workstations. Each position is arranged with individual privacy cubicles, ergonomic seating, clear workstation numbering, and comfortable spacing to ensure complete test confidentiality and eliminate visual distraction between candidates.

Facility Dimension:

250 dedicated candidate stations with wide invigilator inspection aisles.

Power Redundancy

24/7 Power Supply Architecture

Electrical stability is non-negotiable during digital assessments. JOP CBT Center features a tri-tiered power architecture consisting of roof-mounted Solar/Hybrid generation, industrial Diesel generator backup, and central Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) units. This ensures instantaneous failover with zero loss of power or session data.

Redundancy Layers:

1. Solar/Hybrid • 2. Industrial Diesel Backup • 3. Central UPS Grid.

Accreditation Scope

Institutional Accreditation

The facility has been engineered in alignment with technical benchmarks established by major examination authorities including JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and the British Council. Formal accreditation evidence and verification documentation are currently being finalized prior to operational deployment.

Accreditation Status: In Progress

Target bodies: JAMB • WAEC • NECO • British Council. Official documentation will be published upon final inspection approval.

Security & Surveillance

CCTV & Restricted Access

Comprehensive closed-circuit television (CCTV) coverage spans the entire assessment floor, entrance gates, holding areas, and administrative corridors. Controlled perimeter access ensures that only authenticated candidates, proctors, and authorized examination officials are granted hall entry during active test sessions.

Access Control:

Continuous video recording and physical checkpoint screening.

Identity Safeguards

Biometric Candidate Verification

To eliminate candidate impersonation and guarantee test authenticity, our facility is equipped with biometric verification infrastructure. Identity authentication takes place at entry checkpoints before seating allocation, ensuring that only legitimately registered candidates sit for scheduled examinations.

Fraud Prevention:

Multi-stage identity check matching exam registration records.

High-Speed Connectivity

Uninterrupted Dedicated Internet

Digital testing applications require constant, low-latency connectivity to external examination servers. JOP CBT Center operates dedicated, high-availability internet infrastructure supported by structured local gigabit networking. Redundant routing paths safeguard against network drops during live testing.

Network Topology:

Dedicated synchronous connectivity with low-latency local switching.

Personnel Integrity

Certified Professionals with Integrity

Advanced technology requires ethical, competent human supervision. Our team comprises trained proctors, network technicians, and invigilators committed to maintaining the highest ethical standards. Every staff member is bound by strict non-disclosure, impartiality, and zero-tolerance examination malpractice standards.

Ethical Governance:

Strict enforcement of standardized examination proctoring codes.
